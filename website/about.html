1. Professional & Academic Journey

S M Mehdi Akram is a distinguished Bangladeshi IT professional, entrepreneur, and academic achiever with over 17 years of experience in the information technology sector. Born in Kushtia, Bangladesh, he completed his B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) from Prime University, Dhaka, and further earned his M.Sc. / MCS in Computer Science from the University of South Asia, Dhaka. He currently serves as the President of the Prime University Alumni Association (PULLA) for the 2025–2026 term.

As the Founder and CEO of Royal Technologies (established in 2008, with offices in Kushtia and Dhaka), he has spearheaded a comprehensive range of IT solutions including web design, web hosting, domain registration, search engine optimization, IT consulting, and custom software development.

As a prolific bilingual technology writer, he has authored columns for the Daily Amar Desh and maintains both the English Royal Tech Blog and the Bengali technology blog সমকাল দর্পণ (Shamokal Darpon). He is also an active contributor across open digital platforms including WordPress, Fiverr, and GitHub.

3. About This Digital Legacy Platform

This archive was engineered under the principle of radical digital independence to guarantee that all of Mehdi Akram's published writings from Royal Technologies and সমকাল দর্পণ remain accessible forever—even if original domains, hosting servers, or CMS platforms disappear.

  • Zero Server Dependencies: Operates entirely as static HTML/CSS/JS without PHP or MySQL servers.
  • Tri-Layer Redundancy: Every article is simultaneously preserved in an ACID SQLite 3 database (with FTS5 Bengali & English search), clean UTF-8 Markdown files, and deterministic JSON exports.
  • 3-2-1 Preservation Standard: Replicated across public GitHub history, automated cloud backups, and offline external storage.
