CamStudio is freeware Desktop Streaming Video Software. It is able to record all screen and audio activity on your computer desktiop. And save the video to AVI or SWF format.  
By using CamStudio user can create video tutorials for school, college class, record a recurring problem with your computer so you can show technical support people, create video-based information products you can sell, record new tricks and techniques you discover on your favorite software program, before you forget them.
