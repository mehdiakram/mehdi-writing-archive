[Google plus](http://blog.royaltechbd.com/tag/google-plus) is a one of the most popular social networking site by Google inc. In [Google plus](https://plus.google.com/u/0/111670173570585456902) user can tag you without your permission. For that reason you will get notification several time by updating or comments. You can remove existing tags or control who will tag you.  
**How remove tags from Photo**  
Click the Photos icon on the left side of the page.  
Click **Photos of you** tab.  
Click the photo to remove tag.  
In right side panel click **\* people tagged** and click the **X** of your name to remove your name tag.  
Click **OK** to confirm. You will also have the option to block the tagger from name tagging you in the future, otherwise close the lite-box.  
![google plus tag](http://farm9.staticflickr.com/8154/7598298340_b5447b5e49_b.jpg)  
**Set circles who tag you**  
First Click the Profile icon on the left side of the page.  
Go to the **Photos** tab.  
Click **Edit Profile** button.  
Add or remove circles and individuals in the **‘Automatically approve name tags when these people tag you’** field. To manually approve all name tags, remove all circles and individuals by clicking the **X** icons.  
Click the **Save** button.  
Click **Done editing** at the top of your profile.
