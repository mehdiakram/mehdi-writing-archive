Kaspersky Security Scan is a free Anti-Virus Scan program that is making use of Kaspersky’s commercial antivirus engine to scan a system for threats. So you need not to purchase a commercial license for kaspersky or other antivirus.  
This tools detects viruses, Trojan programs, worms, malicious objects, spyware and other threats that compromise your PC’s security. It alerts you to application and software vulnerabilities that can put you at risk and offers smart scanning for optimal PC performance
