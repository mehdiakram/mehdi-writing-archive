SkyFile is an absolutely nice File Sharing web site that will let you have your own file bank in the cloud for free. It is also have ‘file locker’ service for downloading and sharing large files. The free version of SkyFile provides 5 GB of storage for free.  
![SkyFile](http://farm8.staticflickr.com/7271/7521252472_cdf92cab34_b.jpg)  
In SkyFile no complex menus or options. Just upload, download and share a link to a file. That’s the whole thing, and it’s absolutely elegant in how simple and easy it is. Even the confirmation email they send simply has ‘yo’ as the subject and contains very little besides the registration info.  
The website of SkyFile is [www.skyfile.co](http://www.skyfile.co/) and details [http://blog.skyfile.co](http://blog.skyfile.co/)
