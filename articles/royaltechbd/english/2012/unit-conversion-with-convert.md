Convert is a freeware and Portable unit conversion software that will convert the most popular units of volume, time, distance, temperature, density, pressure, speed, mass, power, energy and many others, including custom conversions!  
![convert](http://farm8.staticflickr.com/7233/7405908124_9134228c32.jpg)  
Download convert from <http://joshmadison.com/convert-for-windows/>.  
Convert runs on the all windows version and Linux/UNIX/anything that runs Wine 1.
