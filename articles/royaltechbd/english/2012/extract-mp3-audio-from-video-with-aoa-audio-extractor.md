AoA Audio Extractor is a freeware audio extractor, which provides you a handy tool to extract audio or background music from video files. AoA Audio Extractor extract audio tracks from AVI, DAT, MPEG, MPG, FLV, WMV, MOV, MP4, 3GP video files to MP3, WAV and AC3 audio formats.  
![](http://farm9.staticflickr.com/8027/7477507774_ca55785a04_b.jpg)  
It can also be used to save any portion of the audio of a video file. Download AoA Audio Extractor from [www.aoamedia.com](http://www.aoamedia.com/audioextractor.htm)
