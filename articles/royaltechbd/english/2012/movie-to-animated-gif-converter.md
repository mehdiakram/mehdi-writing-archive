Movie to Animated GIF Converter is a freeware software to convert WMV, AVI, MPEG, and MP4 video and convert a GIF file. To convert video (“File” -> “Open”), seeking to the position in the video where you want to start the animation sequence (use the track bar at the bottom), setting start and end points (buttons on sides of trackbar) and then exporting (“Export” -> “Export to animated GIF…”).
