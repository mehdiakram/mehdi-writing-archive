In SaaS, hosting, agency, and membership websites, visitors want to compare Monthly vs. Annual billing or standard vs. enterprise packages without scrolling through cluttered pages. Yet most Elementor toggle widgets force you to design two separate saved templates before you can even connect a switch.

In Part 2 of our new widget series, meet the **Content Toggle** widget inside **Royal Addons for Elementor**. Free out of the box, it combines a customizable dual-panel switcher with a **built-in responsive Pricing Table builder**, **6 modern card styles**, and **10 one-click presets**—so you can launch conversion-focused comparisons in seconds.

![Royal Content Toggle Widget for Elementor showing Monthly vs Annual segmented switch and 6 pricing card styles](https://www.royaltechbd.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/featured-royal-content-toggle.jpg)

### What Makes the Royal Content Toggle Widget Different?

Instead of acting as an empty shell, Royal Content Toggle works immediately on drop while remaining flexible enough for any custom layout.

#### 1. 10 One-Click Quick Presets

Open **Quick Presets (10 Templates)** at the top of the Content tab and click **Apply Pricing / Toggle Preset** to populate switch labels, pricing tiers, card styling, and color palettes instantly:

- SaaS 3-Tier Pricing (Monthly / Annual)
- Classic Price Table Ribbon
- Header Band & Corner Ribbon
- Dark Glass Tech & AI Plans
- Cloud & NVMe Hosting (1-Year / 3-Year)
- 2-Column Starter vs. Pro (Monthly / Lifetime)
- Agency & Design Services (Project / Retainer)
- BDT Local E-Commerce Plans (Monthly / Yearly)
- Course & Membership Passes (Quarterly / Annual)
- Dual Content Comparison (Standard / Enterprise)

#### 2. 5 Switcher Styles & Promo Discount Badges

Customize the top switcher bar under **Toggle Switch Settings** with five distinct interaction styles: **Round (Pill Switch)**, **Square (Box Switch)**, **Flip Button**, **Material Clean**, and a sleek **Segmented Pill Bar**. You can set alignment, choose the default active panel, attach custom Elementor icons to either label, and display a promo badge (like *“Save 20%”*) after the primary or secondary label.

#### 3. Built-in Pricing Table Grid with 6 Visual Card Styles

When either panel uses the **Pricing Table (Built-in)** mode, choose from 6 crafted card designs under **Pricing Table Style & Layout**:

- **Style 1 — Modern Elevated Card**
- **Style 2 — Classic Corner Ribbon (Price Table)**
- **Style 3 — Solid Header Band & Diagonal Ribbon**
- **Style 4 — Dark Tech Glassmorphism**
- **Style 5 — Gradient Hero Header**
- **Style 6 — Minimalist Editorial Outline**

Set responsive **Columns Per Row** (1–4 columns independently on desktop, tablet, and mobile), adjust card gaps, align content left or center, and place corner ribbons on either side.

#### 4. Rich Plan Repeater with Smart Check/Cross Syntax

Each pricing plan supports a Title, Subtitle/Tagline, Currency Symbol, Price, **Original Price (Strikethrough)** for showing annual discounts, Billing Period, Features Heading, CTA Button text and URL, Featured elevation toggle, Corner Badge/Ribbon text, and an optional custom accent color. In the features textarea, simply prefix any line with `+`  for an included checkmark icon or `-`  for an excluded cross icon—rendered with crisp inline SVGs.

#### 5. Independent Panel Modes & Accessibility

Not building a pricing table? Both **Primary Content** and **Secondary Content** can independently use **Pricing Table (Built-in)**, **Custom Content / HTML (WYSIWYG)**, or any **Elementor Saved Template**—making it easy to mix templates with custom comparison tables. Everything also supports keyboard navigation (Enter/Space) and `aria-pressed` accessibility attributes.

### Who Should Use Content Toggle?

- **SaaS & Software Teams:** Showcase Monthly vs. Annual savings with strikethrough anchor pricing and *“Most Popular”* badges.
- **Hosting & Local Businesses:** Use dedicated presets like Cloud & NVMe Hosting or BDT Local E-Commerce Plans without rebuilding layouts from scratch.
- **Agencies & Freelancers:** Toggle between One-Time Project scope and Monthly Retainer packages.
- **Course Creators & Membership Sites:** Compare Quarterly vs. Annual access or display Before/After feature breakdowns.

### Get Started in Minutes

1. **Drag & Drop Content Toggle:** Search for **Content Toggle** in the Elementor panel and place it on your pricing or comparison section.
2. **Load a Preset (Optional):** Pick any template from **Quick Presets (10 Templates)** and click **Apply Pricing / Toggle Preset**.
3. **Configure Your Switcher & Plans:** Choose one of the **5 Switch Styles**, set Primary/Secondary labels and discount badge, then customize your **Pricing Card Style** and plan features (`+`  / `-` ).
4. **Fine-Tune in the Style Tab:** Adjust switcher track/knob colors, card shadows, typography, check/cross icon colors, and CTA button hover effects, then hit **Publish**.
