#### Customer Management

Store customer profiles, contact details, measurements, and complete order history for faster repeat orders and better service.

#### Measurement Management

Save customizable measurement fields accurately, enabling quick retrieval and consistent fitting for every future tailoring order.

###

#### Order & Job Cards

Create orders instantly, assign tasks, generate detailed job cards, and monitor production progress until final delivery.

#### Design Gallery

Showcase dress styles and design catalogs, helping customers quickly choose their preferred fashion and customization options.

###

#### Trial Management

Schedule fitting appointments, record customer feedback, and ensure perfect garment adjustments before final delivery every time.

#### Fabric Management

Manage customer supplied fabrics and in shop fabric sales with complete tracking and accurate inventory records.

#### Payroll & Wages

Calculate tailor wages using piece rate or monthly salary with accurate payroll processing and payment records.

###

#### Invoice & Billing

Generate professional invoices, manage advance payments, track outstanding dues, and simplify customer payment collections efficiently.

###

#### Inventory Management

Monitor fabrics, accessories, and tailoring materials with real time stock tracking to prevent shortages and wastage.

#### Expense Management

Record daily operational expenses, material purchases, and business costs for improved financial control and profitability analysis.

#### Cloud Backup & Security

Protect valuable business data with automated backups, secure access, and reliable cloud storage options.

#### Advanced Reporting

Analyze profits, expenses, sales trends, inventory valuation, and business performance through detailed reports.

Protect sensitive legal information with secure user roles, permission management, data encryption, and detailed activity logging.
