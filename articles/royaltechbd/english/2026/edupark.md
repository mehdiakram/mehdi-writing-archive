# EduPark - School Management System

EduPark is a powerful, all-in-one School Management System built on Laravel 11, designed to digitize every aspect of school administration — from student admissions to staff payroll, from online classes to fee payments. It eliminates paperwork, reduces manual errors, and empowers schools to focus on what matters most: education.

###### COMPREHENSIVE FEATURES

## Everything Your School Needs

#### Academic Management

Manage classes, sections, subjects, routines, and academic years from one dashboard.

#### Student Management & Admissions

Digitize student lifecycle from online admission application to QR-coded ID cards.

#### Attendance Management

Track daily attendance via manual entry, QR code scan, or biometric device integration.

#### Examination & Results

Generate marksheets, tabulation sheets, admit cards, and seat plans with one click.

#### Fee Management & Online Payment

Automate fee collection, receipts, discounts, and online payments via bKash and Nagad.

#### HR & Staff Management

Manage employee records, leave requests, promotions, and automated payroll calculations.

#### Library Management

Digitize your school library with book issue, return, and overdue tracking features.

#### Hostel Management

Manage hostel blocks, rooms, bed assignments, and hostel fees for residential students.

#### Transport Management

Plan routes, manage vehicles, assign students, and collect transport fees efficiently.

#### LMS & Live Classes

Track company assets, office inventory, equipment allocation, maintenance history, and stock movement with complete accuracy.

#### Communication System

Send bulk SMS, auto email notifications, and internal messages to parents and students.

#### Guardian Portal

Dedicated portals for Guardians to access results, fees, attendance, and more for multiple students.

#### Teacher Portal

Dedicated teachers to access results, attendance, homework, and more.

#### Committee & Alumni Management

Manage school committees and maintain a strong alumni network with contact records.

#### Website Builder

Build and manage your school’s website with sliders, news, gallery, and SEO — no coding needed.

#### Audit Log & Security

Track every system action with full audit logs, RBAC permissions, and data backup support.

#### Multi-Language Support

Switch between Bangla and English instantly with a dynamic language selection system.

#### System Settings & Configuration

Configure SMTP, SMS, payment gateways, demo mode, module toggles, and school branding.

#### Cloud Backup & Security

Protect valuable business data with automated backups, secure access, and reliable cloud storage options.

#### Advanced Reporting

Analyze profits, expenses, sales trends, inventory valuation, and business performance through detailed reports.

#### Role Based Access Control

Protect sensitive legal information with secure user roles, permission management, data encryption, and detailed activity logging.
