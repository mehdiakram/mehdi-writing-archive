###### Got Questions?

**Frequently** *Asked Questions*

Everything you need to know about Royal Addons for Elementor.

### Is Royal Addons for Elementor really free?

131 widgets and 22 extensions are 100% free, forever. No feature locks on the core set. Pro adds 36 advanced widgets and 4 premium extensions.

### Will it slow down my website?

No. Royal Addons only loads scripts and stylesheets for the widgets you actually placed on each page. Unused widgets contribute zero bytes to page load.

### Does it work with Elementor Free and Elementor Pro?

Yes — seamlessly with both. Royal Addons extends Elementor, it does not replace it. All your existing Elementor content and settings remain untouched.

### Is it compatible with any WordPress theme?

Absolutely. Royal Addons is theme-agnostic and works with Astra, OceanWP, Hello Elementor, GeneratePress, Kadence, and any properly coded WordPress theme.

### How often does it get updated?

Very frequently. The plugin is actively maintained with new widgets, bug fixes, and WordPress and Elementor compatibility updates released on a rolling schedule.

### What support is available?

Free users: community forum on WordPress.org. Pro users: dedicated priority ticket support with guaranteed response times from the core development team.
