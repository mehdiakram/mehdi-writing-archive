Every service page, SaaS onboarding section, and agency landing page needs a clear “How It Works” breakdown. When visitors can grasp your workflow in three or four visual steps, hesitation drops and conversions climb. Yet building aligned icons, connecting lines, and responsive mobile stacks manually inside Elementor columns quickly becomes tedious.

In Part 3 of our series, we introduce the **Step Flow** widget inside **Royal Addons for Elementor**. Available free in the plugin and upgraded with **10 modern visual styles**, interactive stepper states, and animated connectors, it turns static process explanations into engaging, responsive step flows.

![Royal Step Flow Widget for Elementor displaying 10 modern process step styles, animated connectors, and interactive stepper](https://www.royaltechbd.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/featured-royal-step-flow.jpg)

### Why Choose the Royal Step Flow Widget?

Instead of juggling separate icon boxes, spacers, and CSS borders, Royal Step Flow manages the entire layout, connector geometry, and responsive stacking inside a single widget.

#### 1. 10 Distinct Visual Styles

Pick a layout preset under **Layout & Style → Step Flow Style** to match your site’s aesthetic instantly:

- **Style 1 — Classic Minimal**
- **Style 2 — Elevated Cards**
- **Style 3 — Floating Top Badge**
- **Style 4 — Watermark Number Cards** (with optional zero-padded numbers like `01`, `02`)
- **Style 5 — Outlined Ring & Progress Glow**
- **Style 6 — Dark Tech Glassmorphism**
- **Style 7 — Gradient Accent Bar Cards**
- **Style 8 — Alternating Zigzag Flow**
- **Style 9 — Compact Inline Pill Steps**
- **Style 10 — Interactive Progress Stepper**

#### 2. Horizontal, Vertical & Animated Connectors

Switch effortlessly between **Horizontal** and **Vertical** directions, with responsive mobile connector formatting built in. Connect steps using **Solid**, **Dashed**, **Dotted**, **Arrow**, or **Hidden** connectors—and turn on **Animate Connector Flow** for dashed or dotted paths to give your workflow subtle directional motion.

#### 3. Flexible Step Markers, Statuses & Badges

Customize each step independently inside the **Steps** repeater:

- **3 Marker Types:** Auto-numbered (`1, 2, 3...`), Elementor **Icon** picker (Font Awesome or custom SVG), or **Custom Text** labels, displayed in **Circle**, **Rounded**, **Square**, or **Diamond** shapes.
- **Per-Step Status:** Mark individual items as **Auto (First Active)**, **Active**, **Completed**, or **Pending / Inactive** to visualize real progress.
- **Badges, Links & Accent Overrides:** Add helpful **Step Badges / Tags** (like *“Step 01”* or *“~5 mins”*), attach optional step URLs with custom link text, and override accent colors per card.

#### 4. Interactive Stepper Mode & Scroll Reveals

Choose how visitors interact via **Interactive Trigger**—let it follow the style default (**Auto**), activate steps on **Click**, highlight on **Hover**, or keep static (**Disabled**). Built-in **Animate on Scroll** uses `IntersectionObserver` to reveal steps smoothly as visitors scroll into view, while automatically respecting reduced-motion preferences and remaining visible inside the Elementor editor.

#### 5. Granular Style Tab Controls

Fine-tune every layer across five Style sections: **Theme & Spacing** (Primary Accent, Secondary Gradient, Completed Step color, and Steps Spacing), **Step Card** (normal/active background, active border, padding, radius, normal and hover/active shadows), **Icon Box**, **Connector**, and **Text & Badges** (including watermark and link colors).

### Who Benefits Most from Step Flow?

- **Agencies & Consultants:** Walk prospects through Discovery, Design, Development, and Launch stages.
- **SaaS & Product Teams:** Present user onboarding flows or API integration milestones with **Style 10 (Interactive Progress Stepper)**.
- **E-Commerce & Local Service Brands:** Explain ordering, pickup, warranty claim, or booking steps cleanly on mobile and desktop.
- **Course Creators & Organizations:** Display curriculum phases or certification roadmaps with badges and estimated durations.

### Get Started in Minutes

1. **Add the Widget:** Search for **Step Flow** in the Elementor panel and drag it onto your page.
2. **Choose Layout & Style:** Open **Layout & Style** to pick one of the **10 Step Flow Styles**, set Horizontal or Vertical direction, select a **Connector Style**, **Icon Shape**, and **Interactive Trigger**.
3. **Customize Your Steps:** In the **Steps** repeater, configure Marker Type, Status, optional Badge/Tag, Title, Description, Link, and Accent Override.
4. **Apply Brand Colors & Publish:** Use the **Style** tab to dial in Accent, Completed, Card, Icon Box, and Connector styling, then publish your page.
