Organize courts, judges, jurisdictions, and legal proceedings with a structured directory for efficient case administration.

Schedule hearings using an integrated calendar with automated SMS, email, and WhatsApp reminders for important dates.

Store, categorize, search, and manage legal documents with version control, secure permissions, and quick document retrieval.

Assign legal tasks, schedule client meetings, monitor deadlines, and improve collaboration across lawyers and support staff.

Maintain confidential case notes, hearing observations, legal research, and personal reminders in a secure digital diary.
