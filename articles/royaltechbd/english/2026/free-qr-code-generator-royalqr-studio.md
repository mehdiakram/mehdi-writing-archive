## Tips for Creating Better QR Codes

A visually attractive QR code is valuable only when people can scan it successfully. Follow these practical recommendations:

1. Use sufficient contrast between the foreground and background.
2. Avoid making the logo too large or covering too much of the code.
3. Choose a suitable export format for your intended use.
4. Test the QR code before sending it to a printer.
5. Make sure the encoded URL is correct and accessible.
6. Keep enough clear space around the QR code.
7. Use a clear call to action so people know why they should scan it.
8. If the code will be printed, test it at the actual size and under realistic lighting conditions.

Following these guidelines will help improve usability across different devices and applications.
