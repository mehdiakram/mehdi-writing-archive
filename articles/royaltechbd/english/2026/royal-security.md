#### Branch & Site Management

Manage multiple company branches and client duty sites or locations from a centralized system.

#### Client Management

Maintain detailed records of clients, including companies and locations where security guards are deployed.

#### Guard Management

Store complete guard profiles, verification documents, employment details, and deployment history.

#### Shift & Duty Roster

Create day/night shifts and assign guards to specific sites with an organized duty roster.

#### Attendance Management

Track daily attendance, absences, late arrivals, leaves, and overtime of security guards.

#### Incident Management

Record, monitor, and maintain reports of incidents, accidents, and security-related events occurring at duty sites.

Manage patrol schedules, monitor checkpoints, and maintain detailed patrol logs for each site.

Register visitor entry and exit, maintain visitor records, and enhance site security through controlled access.

Track company and client vehicles, including entry/exit logs, movement history, and vehicle information.

#### HR & Payroll

Manage employee records, salaries, allowances, deductions, overtime, and payroll processing for guards and staff.

#### Reporting

Generate comprehensive reports, including site-wise duty reports, incident reports, attendance summaries, payroll reports, billing reports, and client invoices.

Protect data with role based access, Passkey login, automated backups, and centralized document management.
