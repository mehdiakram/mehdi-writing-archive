✦ FEATURES

## Everything You Need to Manage Rentals Like a Pro

🏢

#### Property & Unit Management

Create unlimited properties and units. Set floor, type, size, photos, monthly rent, advance amount, utility defaults, and notice period per unit.

👥

#### Tenant Management & Profile

Store tenant info, phone, email, NID, photo, NID scan. Print professional tenant profile PDFs. View from list or individual page.

🔄

#### Tenancy Assignment & History

Assign units to tenants with auto-populated defaults. Track full tenancy history with start/end dates, rent, advance, and settlement records.

💰

#### Rent Collection & FIFO Ledger

Smart FIFO-based payment allocation. Track paid/partial/due status. Add payments, edit charges, and manage rent with a professional interface.

📋

#### Settlement & Checkout

Close tenancies and settle accounts. Deduct utility bills, damages, or other fees from the advance deposit. Print professional settlement statements.

🔔

#### Multi-Channel Notices

Send rent notices via Print (Letter/Slip), Email, and WhatsApp directly from the dashboard. Customizable templates with dynamic placeholders.

🧾

#### Money Receipts & Invoices

Generate and print professional money receipts. Email receipts directly to tenants. Auto-calculated totals with utility breakdowns.

📊

#### Reports & Analytics

Monthly Revenue, Tenant Summary, Tenant Profile, Property-wise Ledger, Profit/Loss — all printable. Powerful dashboard with charts and KPIs.

💼

#### Expense Tracking

Track all property-related expenses with custom categories. View expense breakdowns via doughnut charts and monthly comparisons on the dashboard.

⚡

#### Auto Rent Generation

Set a day of the month and rent entries are automatically generated via WP-Cron. Includes utility bills. Force-generate option available in settings.

🌐

#### Multi-Language & Currency

Support for English and Bangla notice templates. Configurable currency symbol, full name, fraction name, and numbering system (International/South Asian).

⚙️

#### Highly Configurable

Custom labels for Properties/Units/Tenants. Configurable utility bill types. Customizable WhatsApp & Email templates with placeholders. License-based activation.
