### Unlimited Ledgers

Group standard Assets, Liabilities, Incomes, and Expenses easily. Create infinite independent accounts.

### Day Book & Vouchers

Fast data entry via Receipt, Payment, Contra, and Journal vouchers. Keep a 100% chronological record.

### Automated Financials

Instant Trial Balance, Cash Flow, P&L, and Balance Sheet generation without manual calculations.

### Bank Reconciliation

Ensure your system bank balances exactly match your real world bank statements month by month.

### PDC Management

Manage pending Post-Dated Cheques in an isolated register and clear them into your bank later.

Export landscape PDF copies of any ledger, day book, or financial statement instantly for printing.
