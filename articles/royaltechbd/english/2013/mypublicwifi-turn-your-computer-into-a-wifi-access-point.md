MyPublicWiFi is an free virtual WIFI-Hotspot software that turns your laptop/PC into a Wi-Fi access point. Anyone nearby can surf the Internet through your sharing. This is also an ideal solution for setting up a temporary Access Point.  
![MyPublicWiFi](http://farm8.staticflickr.com/7457/9400496240_4f9c0f8c2a_o.jpg)  
The MyPblicWiFi-Firewall can be used to restrict user access to specific servers. You can also prevent the use of certain Internet services (e.g. file sharing programs). MyPublicWiFi allows you to record and track all visited url pages on your virtual WIFI-Hotspot.
