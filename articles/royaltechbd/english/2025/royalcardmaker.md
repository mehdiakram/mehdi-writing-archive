We are thrilled to announce a powerful and dynamic new addition to our Royal Addons for Elementor plugin—the Royal Card Maker. If you’re looking for an innovative way to boost user engagement or need an interactive tool for your next campaign or event, this widget is built just for you.

Imagine your website visitors creating their own custom profile picture frames, greeting cards, or event IDs right on your site, and downloading them with a single click. The Royal Card Maker makes this not just possible, but incredibly easy.

**What is the Royal Card Maker?**  
The Royal Card Maker is a complete card creation studio that you can add directly to any Elementor page. As a site administrator, you set up a base design or template by uploading a transparent “masking image.” Your website visitors can then use this template to:

- Upload their own photo into a designated area.
- Add custom text in two different, fully customizable fields.
- Generate a unique, personalized image ready for instant download.

#### **A Glance at its Powerful Features**

We’ve designed this widget to provide maximum control for the admin while ensuring a smooth and fun experience for the visitor.

**Powerful Controls for Admins:**

- **Custom Masking Image:** Upload any transparent PNG image to serve as the frame or background for the final design.
- **Output Dimensions:** Set the exact width and height of the final downloadable image.
- **Complete Text Control:**

  - **Positioning:** Use simple X/Y sliders to place text fields anywhere on the canvas.
  - **Character Limit:** Set a maximum character count for each input field to maintain design integrity.
  - **Text Box Width & Wrapping:** Define a width for the text box, and long sentences will automatically wrap to the next line.
  - **Alignment:** Align text to the left, center, or right within its bounding box.
  - **Full Typography:** Control the font family, size, weight, and color for each text field independently.
- **Toggle Fields:** Easily enable or disable either of the text fields with a simple switch.

**A Seamless Experience for Visitors:**

- **Simple Image Upload:** An intuitive interface allows users to easily upload a photo from their device.
- **Interactive Image Positioning:** After uploading, users can simply click and drag their photo anywhere on the canvas to find the perfect position—even small images are easy to move!
- **Live Text Input:** Users can type their text and see it appear on the card in real-time.
- **One-Click Download:** A clear download button allows users to instantly save their final creation as a high-quality PNG file.

#### **Who Is It For? (Endless Use Cases)**

The possibilities are limitless, but here are a few ideas to get you started:

- **Social Media Campaigns:** Launch profile picture frame campaigns for a cause, brand anniversary, or national holiday.
- **Events & Holidays:** Allow users to generate custom greeting cards for occasions like Christmas, New Year, or Valentine’s Day.
- **Community Engagement:** Let members of a club or community generate their own virtual membership cards.
- **Conferences & Seminars:** Enable attendees to create their own personalized virtual badges with their photo and name.

#### **How to Use It**

Setting up the widget is incredibly simple:

1. Drag the “Royal Card Maker” widget from the Elementor panel onto your page.
2. In the widget settings, upload your mask image and set the output dimensions.
3. Configure the position, style, and text wrapping options for “Input Field 1” and “Input Field 2”.
4. Publish your page and watch your users get creative!
