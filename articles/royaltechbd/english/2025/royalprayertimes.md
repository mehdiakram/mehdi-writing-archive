For the global Muslim community, knowing the correct prayer times is a vital part of daily life. Whether you run a website for a mosque, an Islamic center, a community portal, or a personal blog, offering this information can transform your site into an indispensable daily resource.

Understanding this need, Royal Tech BD is proud to introduce the new **Prayer Time Widget**, a powerful and feature-rich addition to our popular “Royal Addons for Elementor” plugin. This widget allows you to seamlessly integrate a beautiful, accurate, and automated prayer time schedule directly onto your WordPress site.

### Why the Prayer Time Widget is a Must-Have for Your Website

This is more than just a utility; it’s a tool for building community and trust. When a visitor arrives on your site and finds accurate, location-aware prayer times without any effort, it creates a positive and lasting impression. You are no longer just providing content; you are providing a valuable service.

### Powerful Features at Your Fingertips

The Royal Prayer Time widget is designed to be both powerful and easy to use, offering a range of features to suit your specific needs.

- **Automatic Location Detection:** The widget intelligently detects the user’s location and instantly displays the correct prayer times for their specific area. This provides a seamless, zero-effort experience for your visitors.
- **Manual Location Input:** If a user prefers not to share their location or wishes to view the schedule for another city, they can easily enter a city and country manually.
- **Complete Customization:** Match the widget perfectly with your site’s design. You have full control over labels (e.g., change “Dhuhr” to “Zuhr”), colors, and typography.
- **Two Unique Display Styles:** Choose between a classic vertical list (**Style 1**) or a modern, horizontal layout (**Style 2**) to best fit your page design.
- **Current Prayer Highlight:** The widget automatically highlights the current prayer time, making it easy for users to see which prayer is next at a glance.
- **Live Local Clock:** Along with prayer times, you can display a live clock showing the correct current time for the selected location, adding another layer of utility.
- **Multiple Calculation Methods:** Serve a global audience by choosing from over a dozen internationally recognized calculation methods, including ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura (Makkah), and many more.
