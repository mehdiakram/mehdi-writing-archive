A toll-free number is a telephone number that allows customers to contact an organization without paying any call charges. The receiving organization bears the cost of the call instead of the person making it.

Toll-free numbers are widely used by businesses, banks, government organizations, hospitals, and customer service departments. They provide a convenient way for customers to ask questions, report problems, receive technical support, and get information about products and services.

In Bangladesh, toll-free numbers are commonly identified by the **0800** prefix. Some government emergency and public service helplines also provide free calling facilities through short codes.

This article provides a list of toll-free numbers in Bangladesh, including government services and private company customer care numbers.

## What Is a Toll-Free Number and How Does It Work?

A toll-free number is a telephone number that allows callers to contact a business or organization without paying the applicable call charges.

Unlike regular telephone numbers, where the caller pays for the call, the receiving organization pays the cost of incoming calls.

For example, when a customer calls a company’s toll-free number to ask about a product, submit a complaint, or request assistance, the company bears the telephone charges.

Toll-free services are particularly useful for:

- Customer care and technical support
- Product and service inquiries
- Banking and financial assistance
- Customer complaints and feedback
- Emergency and public information services

## How to Identify a Toll-Free Number in Bangladesh

In Bangladesh, the 0800 prefix is used for Free Phone Services.

For example:

- 08000-161271
- 08000-300300
- 08000-16221

Calls to these numbers are generally free for eligible callers within Bangladesh, subject to the service provider’s terms and coverage.

**Important:** Not every short-code helpline is toll-free. Always check the official website of the organization to confirm whether a number is free to call.

## Important Information Before Calling

- Check whether the number is currently active.
- Confirm that the number is available from your mobile operator.
- Some numbers may be accessible only within Bangladesh.
- A regular customer care number is not necessarily toll-free.
- Alternative numbers beginning with 096 or other prefixes may have normal call charges.
- Toll-free availability may depend on the operator and service provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. What is the toll-free number prefix in Bangladesh?**

The 0800 prefix is used for Free Phone Services in Bangladesh.

**2. Are all 0800 numbers free to call?**

0800 numbers are designated for free phone services, but availability and coverage should be confirmed with the respective organization and mobile operator.

**3. Can I call a toll-free number from a mobile phone?**

Yes. Many toll-free numbers in Bangladesh support calls from mobile phones as well as landlines, subject to operator support.

**4. Is 999 a toll-free number in Bangladesh?**

Yes. The National Emergency Service 999 provides free emergency calling.

**5. Are 096 numbers toll-free?**

No. Numbers beginning with 096 are generally not toll-free. Normal call charges or applicable service charges may apply.

**6. How can I find a company’s toll-free customer care number?**

Visit the company’s official website and look for its Contact Us or Customer Care section.
