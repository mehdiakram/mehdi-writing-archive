For websites serving a diverse, global audience, displaying a simple date is often not enough. Introducing the Royal Date & Clock widget for Elementor, a revolutionary tool designed to bridge cultural gaps by presenting time with unparalleled relevance and accuracy.

This powerful widget simultaneously displays three distinct calendars: Gregorian, Bengali, and Hijri, ensuring information resonates with a wider audience. It includes a dynamic, location-aware clock that supports both automatic geolocation and manual city entry. Its true intelligence lies in its handling of the Hijri calendar; the date smartly transitions to the next day only *after* Maghrib prayer time has passed in the designated location, a feature that respects Islamic tradition with precision. Its rich Bengali language support extends to the clock, showing culturally specific terms like “পূর্বাহ্ণ” for AM and “অপরাহ্ণ” for PM.

The widget provides extensive styling options directly within Elementor. A single, intuitive alignment control manages the entire widget’s position, while you can choose between horizontal and vertical layouts. You can also meticulously style the color and typography of each calendar and the clock individually. Ideal for global news portals, community forums, or any multilingual site, this is an essential tool for connecting with your audience through culturally aware design.
